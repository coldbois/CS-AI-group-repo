## Week 1

### Progress summary

The first week was particularly demanding, as another presentation was scheduled for the same deadline. This made it challenging to allocate sufficient time to both projects. Despite having to dedicate much of our available free time to this project, we worked to complete the first submission to the best of our ability. Although the submission did not fully meet our expectations, it provided a useful foundation for further improvement.

### Work completed

- Identified the strengths of individual team members to support more effective task allocation in future stages.
- Assigned responsibilities to each team member to improve coordination across the different sections of the project.
- Learned the fundamentals of GitHub and established a basic project workflow to support team collaboration.
- Created individual progress logs and meeting summaries.
- Developed an initial plan for the next stage and distributed tasks among team members.
- Conducted preliminary research and organized the collected content into Markdown files.

### Areas requiring improvement

1. **Content accessibility and quality:** the material was not sufficiently beginner-friendly and lacked supporting images, simple analogies, appropriate activities and practical examples.
2. **Repository organization:** the repository structure was not clear enough, which created a risk of duplicated or overlapping work among team members.
3. **Task requirements:** we were unable to satisfy all the checklists and requirements provided by the teacher.
4. **Team alignment:** not all team members had a clear understanding of the project's overall direction and objectives.
