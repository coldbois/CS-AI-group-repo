# Final Mission: Escape Dandroid's Lab

![Dandroid locked in the lab](assets/dandroid/dandroid-nervous.png)

**Time:** 30 min · **Group:** solo or pairs · Study Guide pp. 163–164

Dandroid is locked in the lab! The door has five locks, and each lock needs **one digit**. Solve all five to get the 5-digit code. You may use any page in this module.

| Lock | Question | Look in |
| --- | --- | --- |

| 5 | Wolf, goat and cabbage: how many crossings are in the shortest plan? | [3.4](Symbolic%20AI%20Fundamentals/19_planning-and-problem-solving/Main%20Theory/19_main-theory.md) |

My code: `_ _ _ _ _`

**Escape code: answers**
| Lock | Digit | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **5** | New, Ready, Running, Waiting, Terminated. |
| 2 | **4** | P3 first ran at time 4, needed only 1 unit and finished at 5. Wait = 5 − 1 = 4. |
| 3 | **7** | Halving 100 takes at most 7 steps (2⁷ = 128). |
| 4 | **1** | C and B come off; A is left. |
| 5 | **7** | The goat has to ride back once. |

The code is **5 4 7 1 7**. The door opens and Dandroid is free. Well done, lab partner!
