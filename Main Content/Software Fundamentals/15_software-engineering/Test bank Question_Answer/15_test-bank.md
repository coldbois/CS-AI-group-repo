## Questions

**T16 (True or False).** In Agile, a team builds software in short cycles called sprints.

**M16 (choose ONE).** What comes first in the software development life cycle?

- A. Deployment (release)
- B. Maintenance
- C. Testing
- D. Requirements (find out what users need)

**O6. Paper Airplane Sprints** (about 100 words, 5 marks)

Two teams build paper planes for a "customer" (the teacher). Team W (Waterfall) plans everything first and tests only at the end. Team A (Agile) works in three short sprints and shows the customer after each one. After sprint 2, the customer says: "The planes must now carry a paper clip!"

(1) Which team copes better with this surprise? Why? (2) Why does testing after every sprint help? (3) Give one project where Waterfall could be the better choice.

Use these words: Agile · Waterfall · sprint · feedback · test

**Q1.** You test `is_leap()` on its own with four years. Which level of testing is that?

**Q2.** In Git, what is the difference between a commit and a branch?

**Q3 (True or False).** In Agile, teams get user feedback after each sprint. *(Chapter 2 quick quiz)*

## Answer key

| No. | Answer | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| T16 | **True** | After each sprint, the team shows users the result and improves it. |
| M16 | **D** | You must know what users need before you plan. Testing comes after building, release near the end, maintenance last. |
| Q1 | Unit test | One small piece, tested on its own. |
| Q2 | Snapshot vs line of work | A commit is a saved snapshot of the project; a branch is a separate line of work made of commits, which can later be merged. |
| Q3 | **True** | That's what the sprint review is for. |

**O6 sample answer (125 words).** Team A (Agile) copes better. They show the customer a plane after every sprint, so they hear about the paper clip early and only need to change their design for the last sprint. Team W (Waterfall) made the whole plan at the start and only tests at the end, so they find out too late, and their planes might not fly with a paper clip. Testing after every sprint helps because problems are found early, when they are small and easy to fix, and the customer's feedback keeps the team building the right thing. Waterfall can be the better choice when the plan is clear and will not change, for example software for a lift or a medical machine that must follow strict safety rules.

**O6 key points (1 mark each):** Agile copes better · because they get feedback after each sprint · the Waterfall team finds out too late · testing often finds problems early, when they are easy to fix · a sensible Waterfall example with a reason. *Also accept* other sensible Waterfall examples if the requirements are fixed and clear.
