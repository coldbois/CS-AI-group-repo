# Topic 19 — References and AI Usage

## References

1. UC Berkeley, CS 188. **“State Spaces and Search Problems.”** Search-problem formulation and state-space concepts. https://inst.eecs.berkeley.edu/~cs188/textbook/search/ (see section 1.2)
2. UC Berkeley, CS 188. **“Uninformed Search.”** BFS, DFS and uniform-cost search, including their properties. https://inst.eecs.berkeley.edu/~cs188/textbook/search/uninformed.html
3. UC Berkeley, CS 188. **“Informed Search.”** Heuristics, greedy search, A* and admissibility. https://inst.eecs.berkeley.edu/~cs188/textbook/search/informed.html
4. UC Berkeley, CS 188. **“Constraint Satisfaction Problems.”** Variables, domains and constraints. https://inst.eecs.berkeley.edu/~cs188/textbook/csp/csps.html
5. AIspace, University of British Columbia. **“Planning.”** STRIPS action descriptions, preconditions, add lists and delete lists. https://www.cs.ubc.ca/labs/lci/AIspace/cs322/planning/help/general.shtml
6. Fikes, R. E. and Nilsson, N. J. (1971). **“STRIPS: A New Approach to the Application of Theorem Proving to Problem Solving.”** *Proceedings of the 2nd International Joint Conference on Artificial Intelligence*. https://ijcai.org/proceedings/1971/63

## What each source supports

- Use UC Berkeley CS188 for the search algorithms, heuristic formula and CSP definitions.
- Use UBC AIspace and the original Fikes and Nilsson paper for STRIPS action representation.
- The worked examples in this file are simplified teaching examples created for this module; they are not copied from the source pages.
