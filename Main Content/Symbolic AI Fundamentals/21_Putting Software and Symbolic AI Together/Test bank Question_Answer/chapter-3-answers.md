## Section C — Open-ended Marking Guidance

Answers may vary. Award credit for accurate concepts, a clear explanation, a relevant example and reasoned evaluation. The points below are key elements, not the only acceptable wording.

### Question 1

- Identify a realistic user input and the output.
- Describe the knowledge base and at least two clear rules.
- Explain the order of processing or inference.
- Include an edge case such as missing, invalid or conflicting input and a safe response.
- Mention validation, testing and the limits of the example.

### Question 2

- Explain the neural component’s role in pattern recognition or learning from data.
- Explain the symbolic component’s role in explicit facts, logic, constraints or traceable rules.
- Describe how the neural output is transformed into structured symbols usable by the reasoner.
- Discuss challenges such as recognition errors, uncertain outputs, mismatched representations, rule coverage or evaluation.
- Make clear that architectures vary and the hybrid does not automatically guarantee correctness.
