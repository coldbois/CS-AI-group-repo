# 1.7 Test Bank: Security and Access Control

T7, M7 and O3 are the same as in the *Dandroid's Logic Lab* Test Bank. Q1–Q3 are extra practice. Answers are at the bottom.

## Questions

**T7 (True or False).** Typing your password to log in is called authorization.

**M7 (choose TWO).** Which two are warning signs of a fake (phishing) email?

- A. It says "Have a nice weekend"
- B. "Your account will close in 1 hour!"
- C. It asks you to type your password
- D. Your teacher reminds you about tomorrow's class

**O3. Spot the fake email** (about 100 words, 5 marks)

Dandroid gets this email from support@micros0ft-help.example: "URGENT!! Your account will be CLOSED in 1 hour. Click here and type your password."

(1) Find three warning signs in this email. (2) What should Dandroid do instead of clicking? (3) Explain authentication and authorization with an example.

Use these words: phishing · password · urgent · authentication · authorization

**Q1.** A school website goes down the night before an assignment is due. Which CIA goal is broken?

**Q2.** What can the group do with a file whose permissions are `-rw-r--r--`?

**Q3 (True or False).** Authentication checks who you are; authorization checks what you may do. *(Chapter 1 quick quiz)*

---

## Answer key

| No. | Answer | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| T7 | **False** | Typing a password proves who you are. Correct version: it is called authentication. Authorization is what you are allowed to do after you log in. |
| M7 | **B, C** | Scammers make you hurry so you don't think (B), and real services never ask for your password by email (C). A and D are normal messages. |
| Q1 | **Availability** | The site doesn't work when it is needed. |
| Q2 | Read only | The group's three characters are `r--`. |
| Q3 | **True** | Who you are first, then what you may do. |

**O3 sample answer (129 words).** This email has several warning signs. First, the sender address is strange: it uses a zero instead of the letter "o" (micros0ft) to look like a real company. Second, it tries to make Dandroid panic with an urgent threat ("CLOSED in 1 hour"). Third, it asks for a password, and real companies never ask for your password by email. Dandroid should not click the link. Instead, it should delete the email or report it, and if it is worried, open the real website by typing the address itself. Authentication means proving who you are, for example typing your password or using your fingerprint to log in. Authorization is what you are allowed to do after that, for example being allowed to read a class file but not change it.

**O3 key points (1 mark each):** three warning signs · says not to click; delete or report · visit the real website directly if worried · authentication = proving who you are, with an example · authorization = what you are allowed to do, with an example. *Also accept* other correct warning signs, such as shouting in capital letters.
